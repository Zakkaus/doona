Added

- A time format setting next to the date format. 24-hour is the default; 12-hour writes AM or PM in the interface language's own words (4:27 PM, 下午4:27); Automatic follows the clock of the browser's region. It applies to every time on a page, including the chart axes and the log heatmap, and composes with each date format. Source cards, the log and event time columns and the log heatmap give a 12-hour time the room it needs instead of cutting it short.
